# v2.0.2 — Calibrated helicopter rotor motion

v2.0.2 replaces the inherited stopped-blade helicopter effect with aircraft-specific high-RPM main and tail-rotor animation while preserving the approved 110×110 compact fleet.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the animated HEMS, Police, Coastguard rescue and large Coastguard rescue helicopters from blade-free production bases.
- Calibrated an explicit main hub, rotor-disc footprint, perspective tilt and tail-rotor centre for each airframe.
- Confined HEMS and Police tail motion to their physical fenestron openings.
- Centred both Coastguard tail animations on their exposed mechanical hubs.
- Reconstructed fuselage and tail texture hidden beneath the former stopped blades so no frozen cross or erased-body artefact remains.
- Preserved each aircraft’s eighteen-frame aviation-light sequence, 110×110 canvas, bottom-centre map anchor and infinite APNG loop.

## Rotor outcome

The four aircraft now use continuous translucent rotor discs with moving high-speed sweep highlights. The effect reads as rotation at live-map scale without dominating labels or obscuring the airframe. Tail-rotor motion follows the physical mechanism of each aircraft: clipped internal motion for fenestrons and restrained visible blade accents for exposed rotors.
