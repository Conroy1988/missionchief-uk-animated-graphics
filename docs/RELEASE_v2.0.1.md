# v2.0.1 — Compact live-map scale

v2.0.1 corrects the production display scale of the complete direction-neutral fleet after live-map observation showed that the original 200×200 exports dominated roads, labels and nearby incidents.

## What changed

- All 117 static PNGs and all 117 response APNGs now use a 110×110 MissionChief canvas.
- The complete canvas is reduced uniformly to 55%, preserving the raised three-quarter perspective, bottom-centre anchor and real-world size relationships between every class.
- The 117 approved 200×200 v2.0.0 masters remain untouched as the high-resolution source of truth.
- Every APNG is rebuilt from the original master and its master-coordinate emergency-light fixtures before the completed frame is downsampled once.
- Near-transparent Lanczos ringing pixels are normalised after downsampling so builds remain deterministic without damaging visible body edges or restrained light bloom.

## Map-scale outcome

Across all 117 assets, the visible median footprint falls from 135×91 to approximately 75×51 pixels. The maximum static footprint falls from 188×146 to approximately 104×81 pixels.

## Emergency lighting and motion

- 111 road, pod, trailer, cycle and specialist APNGs retain twelve full-canvas frames.
- Four aircraft and two marine APNGs retain eighteen full-canvas frames.
- Physical lamp cores, compact inner flare and restrained bloom are rendered in the 200×200 master coordinate space, then downsampled with the vehicle.
- Flash visibility is rejected when it is too small or dim at native 110×110 map scale, 75% scale or 50% scale.
- Aircraft rotor traces, aviation lights, marine navigation lights and wake motion remain enabled.
