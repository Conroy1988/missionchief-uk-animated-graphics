# v2.0.3 — Recognisable driven-appliance cabs

v2.0.3 corrects three fire-service sprites that inherited a rear-only equipment-body view and therefore appeared to move without a driver’s cab. F/WrC, WrL CAFS and RP CAFS are now complete driven appliances with an unmistakable front at MissionChief’s native 110×110 map scale.

## What changed

- Rebuilt F/WrC from the approved complete Water Carrier chassis rather than the detached tank module.
- Rebuilt WrL CAFS from the approved complete Water Ladder cab, chassis and ladder geometry.
- Rebuilt RP CAFS from the approved complete Rescue Pump cab and chassis.
- Added restrained F/WrC and CAFS role plaques while preserving the existing UK fire livery.
- Preserved the fixed lower-right raised three-quarter perspective, transparent 200×200 master canvas and deterministic 110×110 export.
- Rebuilt the twelve-frame response APNGs for all three corrected appliances so light fixtures remain physically attached to their new cabs.
- Audited the complete road fleet and left intentional pods, trailers and independently transported equipment unchanged.

## Cab outcome

Each corrected appliance now exposes the visual structure required to read as a driven vehicle:

- windscreen and driver/crew glazing;
- cab roof and physical emergency-light bar;
- front wheel and wheel arch;
- grille, headlamps and bumper;
- a clear boundary between cab and role equipment.

Release evidence includes:

- `cab-legibility-before-after.png` — the rear/module-only inheritance beside each corrected complete appliance;
- `cab-legibility-live-map.png` — the three corrected 110×110 exports at native pixels on light, satellite and dark-gallery backgrounds;
- `v2.0.3-cab-legibility-report.json` — fail-closed front-region, glazing and export-provenance checks.
